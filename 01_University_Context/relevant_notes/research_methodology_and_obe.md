## 2. Institutional Research Standard Reference

As evidenced by the AIUB student research presentation record (*Dhaka 2024 Conference Presentation Certificate #195 and AIUB Student Research Grant Form*):
1. **Explicit Research Questions**: Hypotheses must be empirically testable with operational definitions.
2. **Reproducible Experimental Setup**: Configurations, seeds, computational environments, and preprocessing steps must be fully documented.
3. **Structured Reporting**: Thesis defense questions and research papers must clearly distinguish between **VERIFIED** empirical evidence, **EXPERIMENTAL** project observations, and untested **HYPOTHESES**.
