# Scope, Boundaries, and Assumptions
# Project: BanglaFactBench

---

## 1. Scope and Domain Coverage

The scope of **BanglaFactBench** encompasses factual claims articulated in Bengali, Romanized Bengali (Banglish), and Bengali-English code-mixed text circulating in digital news and social media.

### Included Domains
1. **Politics & Governance**: Statements regarding official policies, legislative enactments, public expenditures, diplomatic relations, and verifiable political events.
2. **Public Health & Medicine**: Claims concerning disease epidemiology, treatment efficacy, vaccine safety, pharmaceutical approvals, and nutritional advice.
3. **Disaster Management & Environment**: Real-time statements regarding natural calamities (cyclones, floods, heatwaves), emergency alerts, damage statistics, and relief operations.
4. **Finance & Economy**: Assertions concerning commodity prices, banking regulations, foreign reserves, economic growth indicators, and investment schemes.
5. **Science & Technology**: Reports on scientific breakthroughs, technological innovations, cyber security threats, and digital platform policies.
6. **Social Media & Current Affairs**: Viral rumors, manipulated quotations, out-of-context digital content, and popular folklore.

### Excluded Content
- **Purely Subjective Artistic Critiques**: Personal aesthetic judgments (e.g., "Movie X is boring") lacking empirical factual criteria.
- **Private Personal Communications**: Personal disputes, interpersonal relationship claims, or defamatory attacks on private citizens not acting in a public capacity.
- **Unverifiable Metaphysical Claims**: Theological, spiritual, or supernatural assertions that cannot be empirically evaluated using authoritative scientific or historical evidence.

---

## 2. Ethical and Legal Boundaries

1. **Political Neutrality**: The benchmark is strictly an instrument of factual verification research. It does not endorse or oppose any political organization, party, candidate, or ideology.
2. **No Misinformation Amplification**: Toxic, dangerous, or defamatory claims are stored only in anonymized/redacted form for benchmark evaluation purposes. The benchmark is not a publishing platform for rumors.
3. **Medical Advice Disclaimer**: Veracity labels on health claims reflect published consensus from authoritative medical bodies (e.g., WHO, DGHS Bangladesh); they do not constitute independent medical advice.
4. **Data Privacy & Fair Use**: Content collected from public fact-checking archives and news reports is utilized strictly under research fair use standards, adhering to site terms and robots.txt protocols.
