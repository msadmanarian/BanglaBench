# Risk Management & Mitigation Matrix: BanglaFactBench

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-01** | **Data Leakage across Train/Test Splits**: Shared events, identical headlines, or overlapping sources inflate test metrics. | High | High | Implement strict cross-domain, cross-source, and temporal splitting with automated near-duplicate and entity-overlap checking scripts (`10_Analysis/data_leakage_analysis.md`). |
| **R-02** | **Hallucination / Fabrication of Citations or Results**: Agent generating fake papers, DOIs, or performance figures. | Critical | Low | Zero-tolerance anti-fabrication policy. All citations cross-referenced against ACL Anthology, arXiv, IEEE, ACM, or DOI registries. Experiments run via code; unexecuted metrics marked `N/A`. |
| **R-03** | **Copyright / Terms of Service Violations**: Scraped websites prohibiting data mining or commercial redistribution. | High | Medium | Strictly collect from public research datasets, open-access fact-checking archives operating under Creative Commons, or official public statements; record licensing conditions in dataset metadata. |
| **R-04** | **Bengali Linguistic Complexity & Unicode Normalization**: Bengali characters have multiple Unicode representations (e.g., ya-phala vs. ref vs. hasanta), causing spurious tokenization mismatches. | Medium | High | Integrate standardized Bengali Unicode normalizers (`unicodedata`, `bangla` library or NFC normalization) at the data intake pipeline before tokenization. |
| **R-05** | **Annotation Subjectivity & Low Inter-Annotator Agreement**: Nuanced claims (e.g., distinguishing `MISLEADING` from `REFUTED` or `OPINION`) causing annotator disagreement. | High | Medium | Develop granular annotation guidelines with explicit inclusion/exclusion rules, run pilot annotation calibrations, and calculate Cohen's Kappa before scaling. |
| **R-06** | **Compute & GPU Resource Constraints**: Transformer and LLM fine-tuning exceeding local hardware capacity. | Medium | Medium | Maintain lightweight, optimized architectures (distil-models, quantized inference, batch size adaptation, classical baselines); transparently document hardware specifications in reproduction guides. |
| **R-07** | **Ethical Sensitivity of Political & Religious Claims**: Misinterpreting sensitive societal statements causing bias accusations. | High | Medium | Enforce strict neutrality: verify claims strictly against authoritative published evidence without ideological bias; anonymize private individuals; focus on factual verification research. |
